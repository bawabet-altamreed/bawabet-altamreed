🩺 Introduction

Community Health Nursing (CHN) combines nursing practice with public health to promote health, prevent diseases, provide treatment, and educate individuals, families, and communities.

📌 Important Note:
Community Health Nursing focuses on the health of the whole community, not only sick individuals.

🏘 Community & Society

Community

A group of people who share common interests, interact together, and have a sense of belonging.

Society

A larger group of people living together under common laws, culture, and social relationships.

💡 Exam Tip:
Community = Common interests.
Society = Larger organized population.

📚 Community Health Nursing

According to the American Nursing Association (ANA), Community Health Nursing is the practice of promoting and protecting the health of populations using nursing, social, and public health sciences.

📌 ANA Definition is one of the most important exam questions.

🎯 Goals of Community Health Nursing

  • Promote health and well-being.
  • Prevent and control diseases.
  • Improve the quality of life.
  • Reduce health disparities.
  • Provide care and support for individuals and families.
💡 Exam Tip:
Remember:
Promote → Prevent → Improve → Support

📚 Principles of Community Health Nursing

  • Based on community needs.
  • Health education is essential.
  • Community participation is important.
  • Health services should be continuous.
  • Equal healthcare for everyone.
  • Professional relationship with patients.
  • Accurate records and reports.
  • Regular evaluation of health services.
📌 Important:
Health services should be available for everyone without discrimination.

👩‍⚕️ Role of Community Health Nurse

  • Antenatal, intranatal and postnatal care.
  • Child healthcare.
  • Vaccination and immunization.
  • Health education and counseling.
  • Disease prevention and control.
  • Community research.
  • Strengthening community resources.
  • Providing specialized nursing services.

❤️ Health & Disease

Health

According to the World Health Organization (WHO), health is a state of complete physical, mental and social well-being, not merely the absence of disease.

💡 Exam Tip:
WHO Definition = Physical + Mental + Social Well-being

Disease

Disease is any condition that disturbs the normal function of the body and may result from organ failure, infections or other causes.

⚠️ Signs and symptoms are indicators of disease and should never be ignored.

🌍 Determinants of Health

  • Income and social status.
  • Education.
  • Physical environment.
  • Social support.
  • Genetics.
  • Health services.
  • Personal behavior.
  • Gender.
📌 Better education and healthy environments improve overall health.

🌱 Healthy Lifestyle

  • Regular physical activity.
  • Healthy balanced diet.
  • Enough sleep.
  • Reduce stress.
  • Strong family relationships.
  • Healthy social life.
  • Avoid smoking and tobacco.
  • Develop useful hobbies.
💡 Exam Tip:
Healthy Lifestyle = Exercise + Healthy Diet + Sleep + No Smoking.

🛡️ Levels of Prevention

Level Purpose
Primary Prevent disease before it occurs.
Secondary Early detection and treatment.
Tertiary Reduce complications and improve quality of life.
📌 Remember:
Primary → Prevention
Secondary → Early Detection
Tertiary → Rehabilitation

🦠 Infection Prevention

  • Vaccination.
  • Proper use of medicines.
  • Frequent hand washing.
  • Safe food preparation.
  • Protection from insects and animals.
  • Stay home when sick.
  • Do not share needles.
⚠️ Antibiotics should only be taken as prescribed by a doctor.

👨‍⚕️ Healthcare Team

Team Members

  • Physician
  • Registered Nurse
  • Pathologist
  • Pharmacist
  • Radiologist
  • Dietitian
  • Social Worker
  • Spiritual Leader

How to Improve Team Efficiency

  • Effective communication.
  • Training together.
  • Simulation practice.
  • Shared goals.
  • Team collaboration.
💡 Exam Tip:
Good communication is the key to an effective healthcare team.
