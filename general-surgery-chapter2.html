💧 Body Fluids

Water forms about 60% of body weight.

  • Plasma → 5%
  • Interstitial Fluid → 15%
  • Intracellular Fluid → 40%
💡 Exam Tip:
Water forms approximately 60% of body weight.

⚡ Electrolytes

Electrolytes are minerals that regulate body functions.

  • Inside Cells → Potassium, Phosphate, Bicarbonate.
  • Outside Cells → Sodium, Chloride.

⚖️ Water Balance

The body maintains water balance through intake and loss.

  • Water intake.
  • Food.
  • Metabolism.
  • Kidneys.
  • Skin.
  • Lungs.

🚰 Dehydration

Dehydration occurs due to excessive fluid loss without adequate replacement.

Causes

  • Severe diarrhea.
  • Repeated vomiting.
  • Intestinal obstruction.
  • During surgery.

Symptoms

  • Thirst.
  • Dry mouth.
  • Headache.
  • Dry tongue.
  • Low urine output.

Treatment

  • Glucose 5% IV.
  • Glucose Saline.
⚠️ Important:
Replace lost fluids as early as possible to prevent shock.

🟣 Potassium (K⁺)

Normal Level: 3.5 – 5.0 mEq/L

Causes of Low Potassium

  • Vomiting.
  • Diarrhea.
  • Diuretics.
  • Insulin.

Symptoms

  • Muscle weakness.
  • Abdominal distension.

Treatment

  • Oral Potassium.
  • Slow IV Potassium.
💡 Exam Tip:
Normal Potassium = 3.5 – 5.0 mEq/L

🦴 Calcium (Ca²⁺)

Causes of Low Calcium

  • Renal failure.
  • Parathyroid deficiency.
  • Diuretics.

Symptoms

  • Tingling.
  • Muscle spasm.
  • Hypotension.

Treatment

  • Slow IV Calcium Gluconate.

🧂 Sodium (Na⁺)

High Sodium (Hypernatremia)

  • Too much saline infusion.
  • Severe dehydration.

Low Sodium (Hyponatremia)

  • Diuretics.
  • Diarrhea.
  • Heart failure.
  • Renal failure.
⚠️ Important:
Sodium replacement must be slow to avoid brain damage.
