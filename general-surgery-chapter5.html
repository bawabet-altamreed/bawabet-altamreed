🩺 Hernia

Definition:
Hernia is the protrusion of an organ through an opening in the abdominal wall.

📌 Types

  • Internal Hernia.
  • External Hernia.

⚠️ Causes

  • Weak abdominal muscles.
  • Persistent cough.
  • Chronic constipation.
  • Enlarged prostate.
  • Increased intra-abdominal pressure.

🤒 Symptoms

  • Visible swelling.
  • Pain during movement.
  • Disappears while lying down.

🚨 Complications

  • Irreducible Hernia.
  • Intestinal Obstruction.
  • Strangulated Hernia.

💊 Treatment

  • Hernia repair.
  • Mesh repair.
  • Emergency surgery if strangulated.
💡 Exam Tip:
The most dangerous complication is Strangulated Hernia.

🦋 Thyroid Gland

The thyroid gland secretes T3 and T4 hormones.

📋 Causes of Enlargement

  • Simple Goiter.
  • Toxic Goiter.
  • Hypothyroidism.
  • Thyroid Tumor.
  • Thyroiditis.

🔥 Hyperthyroidism

  • Rapid pulse.
  • High blood pressure.
  • Nervousness.
  • Exophthalmos.

Treatment: Medical treatment then surgery if needed.

❄️ Hypothyroidism

  • Slow pulse.
  • Weight gain.
  • Low blood pressure.

Treatment: Thyroxine.

💡 Exam Tip:
Hyper = Fast.
Hypo = Slow.

💧 Salivary Glands

Diseases

  • Acute Inflammation.
  • Chronic Inflammation.
  • Stones.
  • Tumors.

Symptoms

  • Pain.
  • Swelling.
  • Fever.

Diagnosis

  • X-ray.
  • CT Scan.

Treatment

  • Antibiotics.
  • Warm fluids.
  • Surgery if needed.
💡 Exam Tip:
Submandibular gland is the most common site of salivary stones.

🎀 Breast Diseases

Common Diseases

  • Mastitis.
  • Breast Abscess.
  • Fibrocystic Disease.
  • Benign Tumors.
  • Breast Cancer.

Mastitis

  • Pain.
  • Swelling.
  • Occurs during lactation.

Treatment: Antibiotics + Warm compresses.

Breast Cancer

  • Painless lump.
  • Weight loss.
  • Axillary lymph nodes may be enlarged.

Diagnosis

  • Ultrasound.
  • Mammography.
  • Biopsy.

Treatment

  • Surgery.
  • Chemotherapy.
  • Radiotherapy.
⚠️ Important:
Biopsy is the definitive diagnosis of breast cancer.

🥤 Esophagus

The esophagus is a muscular tube that carries food from the pharynx to the stomach.

📌 Common Diseases

  • Achalasia.
  • GERD (Reflux Esophagitis).
  • Esophageal Tumors.

🟠 Achalasia

Symptoms:

  • Dysphagia.
  • Regurgitation.
  • Weight loss.

Diagnosis:

  • Barium Swallow.
  • Endoscopy.

Treatment:

  • Dilatation.
  • Heller's Cardiomyotomy.

🔴 GERD

Symptoms:

  • Heartburn.
  • Acid regurgitation.

Treatment:

  • Avoid fatty food.
  • PPIs (Losec).
  • Fundoplication surgery.
💡 Exam Tip:
Achalasia = Failure of LES to relax.

🍔 Stomach

Peptic Ulcer

Causes:

  • H. pylori.
  • Smoking.
  • Stress.
  • Coffee.

Symptoms:

  • Epigastric pain.
  • Nausea.

Diagnosis:

  • Endoscopy.
  • Biopsy if needed.

Treatment:

  • Triple Therapy.
  • PPI.

Gastric Cancer

  • Weight loss.
  • Persistent pain.
  • Vomiting.

Treatment:

  • Gastrectomy.
  • Chemotherapy.
⚠️ Important:
H. pylori is the most important cause of peptic ulcer.

🧫 Colon

Common Diseases

  • Ulcerative Colitis.
  • Colon Cancer.

Ulcerative Colitis

Symptoms:

  • Bloody diarrhea.
  • Abdominal pain.
  • Weight loss.

Treatment:

  • Medical treatment.
  • Surgery if severe.

Colon Cancer

Symptoms:

  • Weight loss.
  • Bleeding.
  • Intestinal obstruction.

Diagnosis:

  • Colonoscopy.
  • Biopsy.

Treatment:

  • Surgery.
  • Chemotherapy.
  • Radiotherapy.
💡 Exam Tip:
Colonoscopy + Biopsy = Gold standard for diagnosis.

🟡 Gallbladder

The gallbladder stores bile produced by the liver.

Gallstones

Symptoms:

  • Right upper abdominal pain.
  • Nausea and vomiting.
  • Pain after fatty meals.

Complications

  • Acute Cholecystitis.
  • Obstructive Jaundice.
  • Acute Pancreatitis.

Diagnosis

  • Ultrasound.

Treatment

  • Cholecystectomy.
💡 Exam Tip:
Ultrasound is the first investigation for gallstones.

🧪 Pancreas

The pancreas is a mixed gland (Endocrine & Exocrine).

Acute Pancreatitis

Causes:

  • Gallstones.
  • Alcohol.
  • Pancreatic duct obstruction.

Symptoms:

  • Severe abdominal pain.
  • Vomiting.
  • Fever.

Diagnosis:

  • CT Scan.
  • ↑ Serum Amylase.

Treatment:

  • IV Fluids.
  • Analgesics.
  • Surgery if abscess develops.
⚠️ Important:
Gallstones are the most common cause of acute pancreatitis.

🟤 Liver

Liver Abscess

Symptoms:

  • High fever.
  • Right upper abdominal pain.
  • Hepatomegaly.

Diagnosis:

  • Ultrasound.
  • CT Scan.

Treatment:

  • IV Antibiotics.
  • Drainage.

Liver Tumors

  • Benign.
  • Malignant.

Treatment:

  • Surgical resection.
  • Liver transplantation in selected cases.

🟣 Spleen

Causes of Splenomegaly

  • Infections.
  • Blood diseases.
  • Tumors.
  • Portal vein obstruction.

Indications of Splenectomy

  • Trauma.
  • Blood diseases.
  • Platelet destruction.
  • Tumors.
⚠️ Important:
Children under 4 years should receive Pneumococcal vaccine before splenectomy.

🚫 Intestinal Obstruction (Ileus)

Intestinal obstruction is the interruption of the normal passage of intestinal contents.

📌 Types

  • Mechanical Obstruction.
  • Paralytic Ileus.

⚠️ Main Symptoms

  • Severe abdominal pain.
  • Vomiting.
  • Abdominal distension.
  • Constipation.

🩻 Diagnosis

  • Plain abdominal X-ray.
  • Blood electrolytes.

💊 Treatment

  • IV Fluids.
  • NPO (Nothing by mouth).
  • NG Tube.
  • Surgery for mechanical obstruction.
💡 Exam Tip:
Remember the 4 Cardinal Symptoms: Pain • Vomiting • Distension • Constipation.

🔴 Peritonitis

Peritonitis is inflammation of the peritoneal membrane.

📌 Causes

  • Perforated ulcer.
  • Appendicitis.
  • Perforated intestine.
  • Surgical contamination.

⚠️ Symptoms

  • High fever.
  • Severe abdominal pain.
  • Vomiting.
  • Abdominal distension.
  • Rapid pulse.

🩻 Diagnosis

  • Blood picture.
  • Abdominal X-ray.
  • CT Scan if needed.

💊 Treatment

  • IV Fluids.
  • Broad-spectrum antibiotics.
  • NG Tube.
  • Surgery to treat the cause.
⚠️ Important:
Untreated Peritonitis may lead to septic shock and death.
