🔦 Laser Surgery

Laser is a concentrated beam of light produced by radiation and used in many medical specialties.

Uses of Laser

  • Cutting tissues.
  • Destroying tumors.
  • Cauterizing blood vessels to stop bleeding.
💡 Exam Tip:
Laser is called the Light Scalpel because it cuts tissues accurately with minimal bleeding.

🏥 Medical Uses

  • Eye Surgery.
  • Plastic Surgery.
  • ENT Surgery.
  • Dentistry.
  • Brain Surgery.
  • Urological Surgery.
  • Chest Surgery.
  • Gynecological Surgery.

✅ Advantages of Laser Surgery

  • Reduced bleeding.
  • Reduced inflammation.
  • Small or no surgical wounds.

❌ Disadvantages of Laser Surgery

  • High cost.
  • Needs trained staff.
  • Incorrect use may damage tissues.
  • May cause bleeding if directed to blood vessels.

🔬 Laparoscopic Surgery

Laparoscopic surgery is performed through small incisions using a camera and special surgical instruments.

Main Types

  • Laparoscopy (Abdomen).
  • Thoracoscopy (Chest).
  • Arthroscopy (Knee Joint).

Principles

  • Create a surgical cavity.
  • Use Carbon Dioxide (CO₂).
  • Gas volume: 4–6 Liters.
  • Pressure should not exceed 15 mmHg.
  • Camera displays the image on a monitor.
💡 Exam Tip:
CO₂ = 4–6 Liters
Pressure ≤ 15 mmHg

✅ Advantages of Laparoscopic Surgery

  • Small incisions.
  • Better cosmetic appearance.
  • Less wound complications.
  • Less postoperative pain.
  • Short hospital stay.
  • Faster return to work.
  • Better for obese patients.
  • Magnified surgical view.

❌ Disadvantages of Laparoscopic Surgery

  • Expensive equipment.
  • Requires skilled surgeons.
  • Needs special training.

⭐ Common Laparoscopic Operations

  • Laparoscopic Cholecystectomy.
  • Diagnostic Laparoscopy.
  • Bariatric Surgery.
  • Repair of Hernia.
  • Thoracic Sympathectomy.

🫀 Organ Transplantation

Organ transplantation replaces a failed organ with a healthy organ from a living or recently deceased donor.

Examples

  • Kidney Transplant.
  • Liver Transplant.
  • Pancreas Transplant.
  • Heart Transplant.
  • Lung Transplant.
  • Corneal Transplant.
⚠️ Important:
Patients require immunosuppressive drugs after transplantation to prevent organ rejection.
