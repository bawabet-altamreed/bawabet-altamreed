👁️ Eye Anatomy

  • Eye diameter = 24 mm.
  • Outer Layer = Cornea + Sclera.
  • Middle Layer = Iris + Ciliary Body + Choroid.
  • Inner Layer = Retina.
  • Main function = Vision.
💡 Cornea bends light → Retina receives image → Optic nerve sends signals to brain.

👀 Common Eyelid Diseases

  • Blepharitis.
  • Chalazion.
  • Trichiasis.
  • Entropion.
  • Ectropion.
  • Ptosis.

🦠 Conjunctival Diseases

  • Muco-purulent Conjunctivitis.
  • Ophthalmia Neonatorum.
  • Trachoma.
  • Spring Catarrh.
  • Pterygium.
💡 Trachoma is caused by Chlamydia trachomatis.

🔍 Corneal Diseases

Corneal Ulcer

  • Causes: Trauma, Infection, Vitamin A deficiency.
  • Symptoms: Pain, Photophobia, Blurred vision.
  • Diagnosis: Fluorescein dye.
  • Treatment: Antibiotics + Eye patch.

Keratoplasty

Replacement of opaque cornea with healthy donor cornea.

🔬 Lens & Cataract

  • Lens refracts light.
  • Main disease = Cataract.
  • Types: Congenital & Acquired.
  • Treatment = Surgical removal + Artificial lens.
💡 Cataract = Loss of lens transparency.

⚠️ Glaucoma

Increase in intraocular pressure causing optic nerve damage.

Types

  • Congenital.
  • Open-angle.
  • Closed-angle.

Treatment

  • Eye drops.
  • Laser.
  • Surgery.
⚠️ Untreated glaucoma may cause permanent blindness.

🩸 Retina

  • Diabetic Retinopathy.
  • Retinal Detachment.
  • Treatment may include Laser or Surgery.

👓 Refractive Errors

  • Myopia → Concave lens.
  • Hypermetropia → Convex lens.
  • Astigmatism → Glasses or Contact lens.
💡 Myopia = Focus before retina.
💡 Hypermetropia = Focus behind retina.
