🌬️ 1. Bronchial Asthma

📌 Definition

Bronchial Asthma هو نوبات متكررة من صعوبة التنفس نتيجة تضيق الشعب الهوائية ووجود إفرازات لزجة بها.

📌 Causes / Triggers

  • Allergy
  • House dust
  • Bird feathers
  • Animals
  • Fungi
  • Pollen
  • Some foods مثل fish, milk, eggs and chocolate
  • Some medications مثل Aspirin
  • Exercise خاصة في الأطفال
  • Smoke and fumes

⚠️ Symptoms

  • Chest tightness
  • Difficulty breathing
  • Wheezing
  • Severe sweating in severe attacks
  • Exhaustion
  • Cough with phlegm
  • Cyanosis in severe cases
  • Increased pulse
  • Low blood pressure in severe cases

🔬 Diagnosis

  • Chest X-ray
  • Pulmonary Function Tests
  • ABG
  • Complete Blood Picture

💊 Treatment

  • Bronchodilators by inhaler or nebulizer مثل Ventolin
  • IV fluids when needed
  • Bronchodilators مثل Aminophylline
  • Corticosteroids in severe cases
  • Humidified oxygen
  • Antibiotics when indicated
  • Mechanical ventilation in respiratory failure

⭐ Exam Tip - Asthma

ركز على: Wheezing + Difficulty breathing + Bronchodilators + Ventolin

🦠 2. Pulmonary Tuberculosis (TB)

📌 Definition

Pulmonary Tuberculosis هو مرض معدٍ تسببه بكتيريا Mycobacterium tuberculosis.

📌 Risk Factors

  • Crowded places
  • Poor ventilation
  • Poor nutrition
  • Diabetes
  • AIDS
  • Cancer
  • Contact with TB patients

⚠️ Symptoms

  • General weakness
  • Weight loss
  • Loss of appetite
  • Fever
  • Night sweating
  • Persistent cough
  • Phlegm
  • Sometimes bloody sputum
  • Difficulty breathing
  • Chest pain

🔬 Diagnosis

  • Chest X-ray
  • Sputum analysis
  • Blood picture
  • ESR
  • Tuberculin test
  • Culture and sensitivity
  • PCR / BacteCT test

💊 Treatment & Nursing Care

  • Rest
  • Healthy diet
  • Vitamin B6
  • Smoking cessation
  • Isolation when the patient is infectious
  • Control associated diseases
  • Anti-TB medications such as INH, Ethambutol, Rifampicin and Pyrazinamide
  • Monitor vital signs, weight and appetite

⭐ Exam Tip - TB

احفظ العلاقة دي:

TB → Mycobacterium tuberculosis
Night Sweating + Weight Loss + Cough
Sputum Analysis → Important diagnostic test

🫁 3. Pneumonia

📌 Definition

Pneumonia هو التهاب حاد في فص واحد أو أكثر من فصوص الرئة.

📌 Methods of Infection

  • Air contaminated with bacteria
  • Contaminated medical equipment
  • Oxygen masks
  • Laryngeal tubes

⚠️ Symptoms

  • Sudden fever
  • Chills
  • Increased pulse
  • Increased respiratory rate
  • Dry cough that may become productive
  • Mucoid sputum
  • Sometimes blood in sputum
  • Difficulty breathing
  • Acute chest pain

🚨 Complications

  • Spread of infection to other lung lobes
  • Respiratory failure
  • Spread to other organs
  • Acute heart failure

🔬 Diagnosis

  • Chest X-ray
  • Blood picture
  • ESR
  • Sputum analysis

💊 Treatment

  • Bed rest
  • Humidified oxygen
  • Antipyretics
  • Broad-spectrum antibiotics
  • IV fluids when necessary
  • Treat heart failure if present
  • Monitor vital signs

⭐ Exam Tip - Pneumonia

أهم كلمات تربطها بـ Pneumonia:

Fever + Chills + Cough + Sputum + Chest Pain

🚬 4. COPD & Pulmonary Emphysema

📌 Main Causes

  • Smoking cigarettes
  • Cigars
  • Shisha
  • Exposure to smoke
  • Polluted dust
  • Repeated microbial infection

⚠️ Symptoms

  • Persistent cough
  • Large amounts of viscous sputum
  • Difficulty breathing
  • Wheezing
  • Cyanosis
  • Increased respiratory rate

🔬 Diagnosis

  • Chest X-ray
  • Pulmonary Function Tests
  • Blood gases

💊 Treatment

  • Complete smoking cessation
  • Avoid smoke and polluted air
  • Cough medications
  • Antibiotics when indicated
  • Humidified oxygen as prescribed
  • Bronchodilators such as Ventolin
  • Aminophylline

⭐ Exam Tip - COPD

أهم Risk Factor:
🚬 Smoking

وأهم نصيحة علاجية:
Stop Smoking

🧠 Quick Review

  • Asthma: Wheezing + Bronchoconstriction
  • TB: Mycobacterium tuberculosis + Night Sweating + Weight Loss
  • Pneumonia: Fever + Cough + Sputum
  • COPD: Smoking + Chronic Cough + Dyspnea
